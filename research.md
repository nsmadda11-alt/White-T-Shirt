## Portfolio Career Exploration Research

### Research Question
What technical, workflow, and documentation capabilities do employers require for entry-to-mid-level AI Solutions Engineer and Forward Deployed AI Engineer roles, and what concrete portfolio features demonstrate those capabilities?[cite: 4]

### Evidence vs. Assumptions
- **Verified Evidence from Postings:** Both roles require building and deploying end-to-end AI applications, rapid prototyping, collaborating directly with business or healthcare stakeholders, and maintaining clean, reviewable code[cite: 1, 3, 4].
- **Assumptions to Validate:** While neither listing mandates a specific static-site framework, employers evaluating technical portfolios assume candidates can trace requirements into working UI/API prototypes and maintain transparent Git commit/issue documentation[cite: 4, 6].

### Comparison of Alternatives
- **AI Solutions Engineer (Everforth):** Focuses more on internal enterprise workflows, system architecture, and integrating AI into existing business operations[cite: 1, 4].
- **Forward Deployed AI Engineer (Charta Health):** Focuses heavily on external client customization, rapid iteration based on client feedback, and translating domain-specific (healthcare) workflows into deployed AI platforms[cite: 3, 4].

### Proposed Feature & Design Decision
Because both roles prioritize rapid prototyping, stakeholder clarity, and documented code decisions, I will structure my portfolio repository with traceable issue-to-commit documentation and build a lightweight asynchronous API prototype demo with a clean user-facing interface[cite: 4, 6].
